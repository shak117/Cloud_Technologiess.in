COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our web development, hosting, and digital marketing services.

How fast can Cloud Technologies deliver my business website? +
For standard business and portfolio websites, we can deliver an interactive working demo within 24–48 hours, with full production deployment in 5–7 business days. Custom e-commerce portals and enterprise web applications typically require 10–14 days.
Can I test a working demo website before committing? +
Yes! We offer a 100% free interactive prototype website built as per your industry demand. You can test the design, navigation, and mobile view on your smartphone before making any financial commitment.
Do your e-commerce websites support UPI payments like GPay and PhonePe? +
Yes, all our online stores include zero-timeout UPI integrations (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM) along with Net Banking and Cards, plus direct 1-click WhatsApp order options for customers who prefer chatting.
What is included in your managed cloud hosting? +
Our managed hosting includes high-speed NVMe storage, global CDN edge caching, 99.9% uptime SLA, daily automated backups, free auto-renewing SSL encryption, and complete maintenance so you never have to manage server settings yourself.
Can I update products and website content myself? +
Absolutely. We build clean, intuitive admin panels allowing you and your team to easily update text, upload photos, edit prices, and manage customer inquiries without touching any code.
